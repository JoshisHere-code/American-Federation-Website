Programs & Services

American Federation is developing programs and services designed to help members grow personally, professionally, spiritually, and within their communities.

🎓 Life Coaching

Life coaching is designed to help individuals identify goals, develop practical plans, build confidence, improve decision making, and move toward the life they want to create.

American Federation plans to offer coaching resources and services as this program develops.

🐕 Dog Training

Our dog-training services are designed to help owners build better communication, obedience, discipline, and relationships with their dogs.

Training opportunities may include puppy training, basic obedience, and responsible dog ownership.

📚 Educational Programs

Education is an important part of building strong individuals. The Federation intends to develop educational materials, workshops, manuals, and other resources covering personal development, life skills, business, leadership, and other useful subjects.

🤝 Community & Member Programs

As membership grows, American Federation intends to develop opportunities for members to connect, collaborate, volunteer, support one another, and participate in constructive community activities.

Members may also have opportunities to contribute their own skills, businesses, and services to the Federation community.

🚀 Future Services

American Federation is still growing. Future programs and services may include events, business networking, publications, additional educational programs, member resources, and other opportunities developed around the needs of the Federation's membership.

Our goal is simple: build useful programs that help our members become stronger, more capable, responsible, and productive individuals.
