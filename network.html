The American Federation Network is designed to connect members, businesses, organizations, and communities around shared values, skills, services, and opportunities.

Individuals

Members can connect with other members, share their skills, develop relationships, discover opportunities, and contribute to the growth of the Federation community.

Businesses

Member-owned businesses can become part of a growing network where members can discover businesses, services, products, and professional opportunities.

Organizations

Organizations and community groups may connect with the Federation as the network develops and opportunities for collaboration become available.

How the Network Works

The Federation Network is built around relationships. Members remain independently owned and operated while participating in a community designed to encourage cooperation, professionalism, personal development, and mutual support.

As the Federation grows, the network can develop into a place where members discover businesses, services, educational opportunities, community programs, and other resources.

Member Businesses

The Federation recognizes that many members have their own businesses, organizations, creative projects, and professional services.
