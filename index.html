WHAT IS AMERICAN FEDERATION?

More Than a Membership — A Brotherhood.

American Federation is a brotherhood and network built for people who believe individuals can accomplish more when they stand together.

Members can join as individuals, groups, or businesses and become part of a growing network based on faith, patriotism, brotherhood, personal development, service, and mutual support.

The Federation connects member-owned businesses and organizations, creating opportunities to collaborate, support one another, exchange services, and build something that benefits the entire Federation.

THE FEDERATION NETWORK

Connected Members.
Stronger Together.

The Federation Network connects members and member-owned businesses, creating opportunities to collaborate, promote, exchange services, and support one another.

  • Member businesses can remain independently owned while participating in the Federation network.
  • Members can collaborate on projects and support one another.
  • Businesses can help promote Federation initiatives and affiliated nonprofit activities.
  • Membership contributions can help fund the Federation's operations, programs, growth, and future projects.
  • Strong relationships create stronger opportunities for everyone.
