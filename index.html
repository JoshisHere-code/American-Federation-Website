WHAT IS AMERICAN FEDERATION?

A Brotherhood. A Network. A Federation.

American Federation is a membership-based brotherhood and network built around unity, faith, patriotism, personal development, service, and mutual support.

Individuals, groups, and businesses can become part of the Federation and participate in a growing network designed to encourage cooperation, opportunity, and community.
