American Federation

About the Federation

American Federation is a developing organization built around brotherhood, responsibility, personal growth, faith, service, education, and a commitment to strengthening individuals, families, communities, and businesses.

Our Purpose

The American Federation exists to bring people together around shared principles, mutual support, personal development, and constructive community involvement.

We are building an organization where individuals can develop themselves, connect with others, pursue opportunities, and contribute their talents toward something greater than themselves.

Our Mission

Our mission is to build a strong and responsible membership community while creating programs, services, educational opportunities, and resources that help our members grow.

As the Federation develops, we intend to create opportunities for members, groups, and businesses to work together and contribute to the continued growth of the organization.

Our Values

  • Faith and spiritual development
  • Personal responsibility
  • Integrity and honesty
  • Brotherhood and mutual respect
  • Patriotism and civic responsibility
  • Education and personal growth
  • Service to others
  • Entrepreneurship and self-reliance

Building for the Future

American Federation is being built step by step. Our long-term vision includes membership programs, educational resources, life coaching, community services, business relationships, publications, events, and other opportunities for our members.

We believe a strong organization begins with strong individuals. The Federation is committed to developing that foundation first and growing from there.
